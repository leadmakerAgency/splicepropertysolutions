Two big advantages

Residency and a friendly tax setting

For many buyers, the investment is only half the appeal. A qualifying property purchase can open a route to permanent residency for the whole family, and Cyprus is well known for its attractive lifestyle and tax advantages.

Investors commonly point to a low headline corporate tax rate, non-domicile benefits on certain income, and the absence of inheritance, gift and wealth taxes. The right structure matters, and it is worth getting professional advice early.
