Tax for movers & retirees

One of the big reasons people come

Cyprus is widely regarded as one of Europe's more welcoming tax environments for new residents and retirees. In broad terms, people talk about:

Non-domicile status

New residents can often qualify as non-domiciled, which is associated with exemptions on certain investment income such as dividends and interest for a number of years.

Pension treatment

Foreign pension income can typically be taxed under favourable arrangements, which is a key attraction for retirees choosing the island.

What is not taxed

Cyprus is commonly noted for having no inheritance tax, no gift tax and no wealth tax, though rules and thresholds always apply.
