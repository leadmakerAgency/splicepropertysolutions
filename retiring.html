A better chapter

Retirement should feel like a reward, not a compromise. Cyprus offers the climate, the safety and the relaxed Mediterranean rhythm to enjoy it, at a cost of living that often stretches a pension further than back home.

Why retire here

The retirement so many picture

Everything that makes Cyprus a joy to live in matters even more in retirement.

Climate

Sun almost all year

Warm, dry summers and mild winters mean an active, outdoors retirement rather than months spent indoors.

Health

Trusted healthcare

A modern national system (GESY) plus excellent, affordable private care and English-speaking doctors.

Value

Your pension goes further

Dining, everyday living and property can cost noticeably less than the UK or Northern Europe.

Safety

Calm and secure

One of Europe's safest places to live, with a gentle pace and a strong sense of community.

Community

A ready-made circle

Long-established expat and retiree communities make it easy to arrive and quickly feel at home.

Access

Home is a short hop

Direct flights and EU membership keep family and friends within easy reach.

Healthcare & GESY

Where retirees settle

Find your gentler pace

Some parts of the island suit a relaxed retirement especially well.

Paphos

A long-time favourite for retirees: UNESCO heritage, golf, resorts and a settled international community.

Larnaca

Easy-going seaside living beside the island's main airport, so family are only ever a short flight away.

Limassol

For those who want more buzz: marina life, culture and dining, with quieter suburbs nearby.

Coastal villages

Slower, traditional Cyprus by the sea, where daily life winds right down.
