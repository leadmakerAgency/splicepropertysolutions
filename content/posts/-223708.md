You can hold a residence permit in Cyprus and still be tax resident elsewhere, or the reverse. For most people deciding between Limassol for business, Paphos for quieter retirement life, or Larnaca for airport convenience, the tax residency decision affects pensions, rental income and dividend treatment.</p>
