<p>Welcome to Cyprus, where the sun always shines and the retirement lifestyle is idyllic. As you consider making the move to this beautiful island, it's important to understand the tax implications on your foreign pension. Here, we break down everything you need to know about Cyprus tax on foreign pensions in plain English.</p>

<h2>What is Cyprus Tax on Foreign Pensions?</h2>
<p>Cyprus is known for its attractive tax system, with many retirees choosing to relocate here for its favourable tax policies. When it comes to foreign pensions, Cyprus has a specific tax regime that applies to pension income received from abroad.</p>

<p>Essentially, if you are a Cyprus tax resident and receive income from a foreign pension, you will be subject to tax on that income in Cyprus. This includes both state and private pensions from outside of Cyprus.</p>

<h2>How is Cyprus Tax on Foreign Pensions Calculated?</h2>
<p>The amount of tax you pay on your foreign pension income will depend on your overall income and tax rate in Cyprus. This is known as the progressive tax system, where the more you earn, the higher your tax rate will be.</p>

<p>The tax rates in Cyprus for 2021 are as follows:</p>

<ul>
  <li>0% tax on income up to €19,500</li>
  <li>20% tax on income between €19,501 and €28,000</li>
  <li>25% tax on income between €28,001 and €36,300</li>
  <li>30% tax on income between €36,301 and €60,000</li>
  <li>35% tax on income over €60,000</li>
</ul>

<h2>Do I Need to Pay Cyprus Tax on My Foreign Pension?</h2>
<p>If you are a tax resident in Cyprus, you are required to declare and pay tax on your foreign pension income. This applies to both permanent residents and individuals who spend more than 183 days in a tax year in Cyprus.</p>

<p>However, if you are a non-tax resident in Cyprus, you will only be taxed on income earned in Cyprus and not on your foreign pension.</p>

<h2>How Do I Declare and Pay Cyprus Tax on My Foreign Pension?</h2>
<p>If you are a Cyprus tax resident, you will need to complete a tax return and declare your foreign pension income. This can be done online or through a tax advisor.</p>

<p>Once your income has been declared, you will need to pay any taxes owed to the Cyprus tax authorities.</p>

<h2>What Are the Benefits of Cyprus Tax on Foreign Pensions?</h2>
<p>While paying taxes is never fun, there are some benefits to Cyprus tax on foreign pensions. Firstly, Cyprus has a double tax treaty with many countries, meaning you will not be taxed twice on the same income.</p>

<p>Additionally, Cyprus offers a generous exemption on foreign pension income of up to €3,420 per year. This means that if your foreign pension income is less than this amount, you will not be required to pay any tax on it in Cyprus.</p>

<h2>Frequently Asked Questions</h2>

<h3>Q: Can I Transfer My Pension to Cyprus to Avoid Taxes?</h3>
<p>Unfortunately, transferring your pension to Cyprus solely for tax purposes is not allowed. This is known as pension liberation and is considered a fraudulent activity.</p>

<h3>Q: Are There Any Other Taxes I Need to Consider in Cyprus?</h3>
<p>In addition to income tax, there are other taxes to consider when relocating to Cyprus, such as property taxes and capital gains tax. It's always best to consult with a tax professional to fully understand your tax obligations in Cyprus.</p>

<h3>Q: Can I Claim Any Tax Deductions on My Foreign Pension Income?</h3>
<p>Cyprus does not currently offer any tax deductions on foreign pension income. However, if you are a tax resident in Cyprus, you may be eligible for certain tax credits, such as the personal tax credit.</p>
