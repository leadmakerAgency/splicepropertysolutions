<h2>Retiring to Cyprus healthcare: how the system works</h2>
<p>Thinking about retiring to Cyprus healthcare is a key part of planning your move, especially if you are based in the UK. You will want to understand how public and private services work, what documentation you may need, and how to keep continuity of care for ongoing conditions. This guide explains the practical steps and local realities, so you can make a calm, informed decision.</p>

<h2>Overview of public healthcare in Cyprus</h2>
<p>Cyprus operates a national system called the General Healthcare System, or GHS, which covers residents for a range of primary and secondary services. To use GHS you typically need legal residency and to register with a local doctor, known as a general practitioner.</p>
<p>GHS often covers GP visits, hospital care, and referrals to specialists, with some co-payments for patients. The system is available across Cyprus, with strong coverage in larger towns such as Nicosia, Limassol and Larnaca, and solid services in Paphos and Larnaca for routine care.</p>

<h2>Using UK documents, S1, GHIC and residency</h2>
<p>If you are a UK national planning to move, your entitlement to state-covered care may depend on your circumstances. Some UK pensioners may be eligible for an S1 form that can cover healthcare costs in Cyprus, but eligibility and acceptance can vary so you should check with the UK Department for Work and Pensions before moving.</p>
<p>For short visits, the UK Global Health Insurance Card, GHIC, can provide emergency and necessary care, but it is not intended for people who are resident in Cyprus. Once you become resident you will typically need to register locally and, if required, contribute to social insurance to access GHS services.</p>

<h2>Private healthcare: choice, quality and costs</h2>
<p>Private healthcare in Cyprus is widely available and often used by expats who want faster access or English-speaking consultants. Major towns such as Limassol and Nicosia host large private hospitals and clinics, while Paphos and Larnaca offer reputable private services suitable for retirees.</p>
