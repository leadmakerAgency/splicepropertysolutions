<p>If you are researching things to do in Cyprus for residents from the UK, this guide will help you picture daily life and leisure after a move. Cyprus blends Mediterranean coastlines, inland villages, mountain trails and a relaxed social calendar, so you can easily tailor life to your pace, whether you plan to work, invest or retire. Below you will find practical tips, local examples and ideas to explore before you commit.</p>

<h2>Getting settled: paperwork, practicalities and everyday life</h2>
<p>Moving to Cyprus typically involves a few practical steps you should plan for, such as residency status, opening a bank account and finding healthcare cover. Rules, required documents and processing times can vary depending on your citizenship, employment or investment plans, so you should confirm current requirements with official sources.</p>
<p>Housing options range from city apartments in Nicosia and Larnaca to coastal villas in Limassol, Paphos and Protaras. You may prefer to rent first to familiarise yourself with neighbourhoods before buying, and a local agent can often help with viewings and legal introductions.</p>

<h2>Outdoor life: beaches, sea and mountains</h2>
<h3>Beaches and watersports</h3>
<p>Cyprus is famous for sandy shores and clear water. Ayia Napa and Protaras offer family friendly beaches and lively summer scenes, while quieter coves near Paphos are better for low-key weekends. Snorkelling, diving and boat trips are widely available, and diving enthusiasts often explore the Zenobia wreck near Larnaca.</p>
<h3>Hiking and the Troodos</h3>
<p>The Troodos mountains give you a change of pace, with cooler temperatures and trails through pine forests and traditional villages. Wine villages like Omodos and Kakopetria are popular for day trips, especially in spring and autumn when the scenery is at its best.</p>
<h3>Golf and outdoor sports</h3>
<p>If golf or outdoor tennis is part of your lifestyle, Cyprus has several golf courses, including larger resorts you can join or visit as a guest. Coastal promenades, such as the Limassol seafront, are excellent for walking, cycling and relaxed outdoor socialising.</p>
