<p>If you are thinking to retire in Limassol, you are considering one of Cyprus most cosmopolitan coastal cities, with warm winters, a lively marina, and easy access to both beaches and the Troodos mountains. From the UK, Limassol often appeals for its walkable seafront, good private healthcare options, and a range of properties from modern apartments to Mediterranean villas. This guide explains practical steps, likely costs, and everyday life so you can research with confidence before you make any moves.</p>

<h2>Retire in Limassol: lifestyle and climate</h2>
<p>Limassol offers a Mediterranean climate with mild winters and long, hot summers, which suits many retirees who prefer outdoor living. You can expect a vibrant café scene, regular cultural events, and a compact city centre that mixes traditional neighbourhoods with modern developments. Many expats choose Limassol for its combination of seaside leisure and good links to airports in Larnaca and Paphos for family visits back to the UK.</p>

<h2>Housing and neighbourhoods: where to look</h2>
<p>Limassol has a wide spectrum of housing options, from renovated apartments in the old town to luxury units around the marina, and detached villas in suburbs such as Germasogeia and Agios Tychonas. If you prefer quieter residential streets, look further from the waterfront where plots and larger gardens become more common. Consider proximity to shops, healthcare and the promenade when you shortlist properties, and visit in different seasons
