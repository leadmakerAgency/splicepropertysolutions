<p>Are you considering a move or investment in Cyprus? The beautiful Mediterranean island has become an increasingly popular destination for retirees, investors, and those looking for a change of scenery. One important aspect to consider when making the move to Cyprus is the country's non-dom status. In this article, we'll break down what this means and how it can benefit you as a potential property owner or resident in Cyprus.</p>

<h2>What is Cyprus Non-Dom Status?</h2>
<p>Cyprus non-dom status is a tax residency scheme offered by the Cypriot government to attract high-net-worth individuals to the country. This status allows individuals who are not domiciled in Cyprus to receive certain tax advantages, making it an attractive option for those looking to move or invest in Cyprus.</p>

<h2>How Can It Benefit You?</h2>
<p>If you are considering moving to Cyprus or investing in property, non-dom status can offer several advantages:</p>
<ul>
  <li>Exemption from taxation on dividends, interest, and rental income earned outside of Cyprus.</li>
  <li>No inheritance tax on assets located outside of Cyprus.</li>
  <li>No capital gains tax on the sale of assets located outside of Cyprus.</li>
  <li>Reduced personal income tax rate of 5% on income earned in Cyprus.</li>
</ul>

<p>Overall, non-dom status can help you maximize your investment in Cyprus by reducing your tax burden and allowing you to keep more of your earnings.</p>

<h2>How to Apply for Cyprus Non-Dom Status</h2>
<p>In order to be eligible for non-dom status in Cyprus, you must meet certain criteria:</p>
<ul>
  <li>You must be a non-domiciled individual, meaning your permanent home or primary residence is not in Cyprus.</li>
  <li>You must not have been a resident of Cyprus for the past 20 years.</li>
  <li>You must maintain a permanent residence in Cyprus, either through ownership or rental.</li>
  <li>You must not engage in any permanent business activities in Cyprus.</li>
</ul>

<p>If you meet these criteria, you can apply for non-dom status by submitting an application to the Cypriot tax authorities. The application must include documentation to support your eligibility, such as proof of non-domicile and details of your Cyprus residence.</p>

<h2>FAQs About Cyprus Non-Dom Status</h2>
<h3>What are the costs associated with applying for non-dom status in Cyprus?</h3>
<p>The cost of applying for non-dom status in Cyprus is a one-time fee of €5,000.</p>
<h3>Can non-dom status be revoked?</h3>
<p>Yes, non-dom status can be revoked if you fail to meet the eligibility criteria or if you engage in permanent business activities in Cyprus.</p>
<h3>How long does non-dom status last?</h3>
<p>Non-dom status is granted for a period of 17 years, after which it must be renewed.</p>
