<h2>retiring to cyprus from uk: practical first steps</h2>
<p>Thinking about retiring to cyprus from uk means planning more than sunshine and sea views. You will want to understand residency, healthcare, taxes and the property market before you commit. This short guide sets out practical steps, local choices and common pitfalls so you can begin sensible research from the UK.</p>

<h2>Residency and legal permissions</h2>
<p>Post Brexit, British citizens no longer have automatic free movement into Cyprus, so you will usually need to apply for a residence permit. Options often include temporary residence for financially independent persons, family reunification, or work and self employment permits, depending on your situation. Immigration rules, required documentation and processing times change, so check with the Civil Registry and Migration Department and consider independent immigration advice.</p>
<p>You may also need to show proof of income, a clean criminal record and health insurance, depending on the route. A local lawyer can confirm which permit suits your plans, whether you want to live in Limassol, Paphos, Larnaca or elsewhere.</p>
