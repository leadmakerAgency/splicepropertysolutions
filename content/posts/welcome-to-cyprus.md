Cyprus has been quietly building its reputation as one of Europe's best-kept secrets for property buyers, retirees, and remote workers — and in 2025, the secret is out.

## What Makes Cyprus Different

Unlike many Mediterranean destinations that have priced out serious buyers, Cyprus still offers genuine value. Property prices in the main expat hubs — Limassol, Paphos, Larnaca — remain competitive relative to Spain, Portugal, or Italy, while the quality of life is arguably better.

Key advantages:

- **Non-dom tax regime** — foreign income from dividends and interest is exempt for up to 17 years
- **Flat personal income tax** — well below the EU average for higher earners
- **EU citizenship route** — long-term residents can naturalise after five years
- **English-speaking country** — one of only a handful in the EU where English is widely spoken at government level

## The Property Market in 2025

Demand from UK, Israeli, Russian, and Eastern European buyers remains strong. New builds are rising, but resale stock in established neighbourhoods still offers the best value — especially if you know where to look.

## Is It Right for You?

The honest answer: Cyprus suits people who want warmth, stability, and simplicity — with a genuine path to long-term residency or citizenship. It's less suited to those looking for constant urban stimulation or nightlife at scale.
