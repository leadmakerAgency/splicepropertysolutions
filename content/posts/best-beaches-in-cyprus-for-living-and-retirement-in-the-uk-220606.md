<p>Cyprus is a popular destination for UK residents looking to move, invest, or retire abroad. And it's no surprise why - with its beautiful beaches, warm climate, and laid-back lifestyle, Cyprus has a lot to offer. But with so many beaches to choose from, it can be overwhelming to decide where to live near. Let us help you narrow down the options with our guide to the best beaches in Cyprus for living and retirement.</p>

<h2>Limassol: The Beach City</h2>
<p>Located on the southern coast of Cyprus, Limassol is known for its bustling city center and beautiful beaches. With a mix of modern and traditional, Limassol offers a vibrant and diverse community for residents.</p>

<h3>Why Live Here?</h3>
<p>Limassol offers a variety of beaches to suit every lifestyle. From the lively and popular Governor's Beach to the quieter and more secluded Lady's Mile Beach, there's something for everyone. Plus, with a range of amenities and activities nearby, you'll never run out of things to do.</p>

<h3>What's the Cost?</h3>
<p>The cost of living in Limassol is typically higher than other areas of Cyprus. However, the benefits of living near the beach and having access to all the city has to offer make it worth the investment. Prices for a two-bedroom apartment near the beach start at around €600 per month.</p>

<h2>Paphos: The Coastal Gem</h2>
<p>If you're looking for a more laid-back and relaxed lifestyle, Paphos may be the perfect fit for you. Located on the southwest coast of Cyprus, Paphos is known for its beautiful beaches, ancient ruins, and charming harbor.</p>

<h3>Why Live Here?</h3>
<p>Paphos offers a slower pace of life, making it a popular choice for retirees. The town has a strong expat community, making it easy to meet new people and make friends. And with beautiful beaches like Coral Bay and Lara Beach nearby, you'll never tire of the stunning views.</p>

<h3>What's the Cost?</h3>
<p>The cost of living in Paphos is generally lower than other areas of Cyprus. A two-bedroom apartment near the beach can be found for around €500 per month. Plus, with a range of affordable dining and entertainment options, you can enjoy the relaxed lifestyle without breaking the bank.</p>

<h2>Larnaca: The Seaside City</h2>
<p>Located on the east coast of Cyprus, Larnaca is a bustling city with a mix of modern and traditional charm. It's home to some of the island's most popular beaches, making it a top choice for those looking to live near the sea.</p>

<h3>Why Live Here?</h3>
<p>Larnaca offers a diverse range of beaches, from the popular Finikoudes Beach with its cafes and restaurants to the more secluded Kapparis Beach. Plus, with a range of cultural attractions and events, there's always something to do in Larnaca.</p>

<h3>What's the Cost?</h3>
<p>The cost of living in Larnaca is generally lower than other major cities in Cyprus. Prices for a two-bedroom apartment near the beach start at around €550 per month. Plus, with affordable dining and entertainment options, you can enjoy the beach lifestyle without breaking the bank.</p>

<h2>Nicosia: The Inland Capital</h2>
<p>Nicosia, the capital of Cyprus, is located inland but still offers easy access to some of the island's best beaches. It's a bustling city with a mix of modern and historic charm, making it a popular choice for those looking to live near the beach but still have all the amenities of a city.</p>

<h3>Why Live Here?</h3>
<p>Nicosia offers a central location, making it easy to explore other parts of the island. And with its proximity to popular beaches like Larnaca and Limassol, you can easily plan day trips to the coast. Plus, the city has a rich history and culture, with plenty of museums, galleries, and events to keep you entertained.</p>

<h3>What's the Cost?</h3>
<p>The cost of living in Nicosia is generally lower than other major cities in Cyprus. Prices for a two-bedroom apartment near the beach start at around €600 per month. Plus, with affordable dining and entertainment options, you can enjoy the beach lifestyle without breaking the bank.</p>

<h2>Frequently Asked Questions</h2>

<h3>Is it easy to get residency in Cyprus?</h3>
<p>Yes, it is relatively easy to obtain residency in Cyprus. EU citizens can live and work in Cyprus without a visa, while non-EU citizens can apply for a residence permit. However, rules and requirements may vary, so it's best to consult with a qualified professional.</p>

<h3>Are there any tax benefits to living in Cyprus?</h3>
<p>Cyprus offers a range of tax benefits for residents, including a low corporate tax rate and no tax on dividend income. However, tax laws are subject to change and can vary for each individual, so it's best to consult with a qualified tax professional.</p>
