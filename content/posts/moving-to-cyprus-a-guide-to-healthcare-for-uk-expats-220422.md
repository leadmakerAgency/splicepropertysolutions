Moving to Cyprus: A Guide to Healthcare for UK Expats

If you are considering moving to Cyprus, healthcare may be one of your top concerns as an expat. It's important to understand how the healthcare system works in this Mediterranean island nation, as well as the options available to you as a UK citizen. In this guide, we will cover everything you need to know about healthcare in Cyprus for UK expats, including details on how to access care, costs, and important considerations to keep in mind. 

Accessing Healthcare in Cyprus

Cyprus has a public healthcare system, known as the General Healthcare System (GHS), which provides free or low-cost healthcare to all citizens and legal residents. As a UK citizen, you are entitled to use the GHS if you are a legal resident of Cyprus. To become a legal resident, you will need to apply for a residence permit and provide proof of health insurance coverage. 

In addition to the GHS, there are also private healthcare options available in Cyprus. These private facilities often offer a higher level of care and shorter wait times, but they come at a higher cost. It's important to note that private insurance is not mandatory in Cyprus, and you are not required to have it to access care. 

Costs of Healthcare in Cyprus

Under the GHS, you will be required to pay a small co-payment for each visit to a healthcare provider. This co-payment is currently set at €6 for a specialist visit and €3 for a general practitioner visit. For hospital stays, the co-payment is €10 per day. 

Private healthcare costs can vary greatly depending on the provider and the type of treatment needed. It's important to research and compare different options to find the best fit for your needs and budget. 

Important Considerations for UK Expats

One important consideration for UK expats moving to Cyprus is whether or not to maintain your UK healthcare coverage. As a UK citizen, you are entitled to free healthcare through the National Health Service (NHS) while visiting the UK. However, if you are no longer a UK resident, this entitlement may change. It's important to research your options and make an informed decision before moving to Cyprus. 

Another consideration is the availability of specific treatments and medications in Cyprus. While the GHS covers a wide range of services, there may be limitations or delays in accessing certain treatments or medications. It's important to discuss any ongoing medical needs with your healthcare provider and make a plan for managing them in Cyprus. 

Frequently Asked Questions

<h2>Frequently Asked Questions</h2>

<h3>1. Can I use my UK EHIC in Cyprus?</h3>
<p>Yes, you can use your UK European Health Insurance Card (EHIC) in Cyprus for temporary stays. However, if you are a legal resident of Cyprus, you will need to apply for a residence permit and access healthcare through the GHS.</p>

<h3>2. Are there any age restrictions for using the GHS?</h3>
<p>No, there are no age restrictions for using the GHS. All legal residents of Cyprus are entitled to access healthcare through the system.</p>

<h3>3. Are pre-existing conditions covered under the GHS?</h3>
<p>Yes, pre-existing conditions are covered under the GHS. However, there may be limitations or exclusions for certain treatments or medications. It's important to discuss any pre-existing conditions with your healthcare provider.</p>
