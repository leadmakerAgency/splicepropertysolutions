<h2>Frequently Asked Questions</h2>
<h3>Do I need a visa to move to Cyprus?</h3>
<p>If you are a citizen of a European Union (EU) country, you can move to Cyprus freely and live and work without a visa. For non-EU citizens, a residence permit is required, which can be obtained through various routes such as employment, retirement, or investment.</p>

<h3>What are the tax implications of moving to Cyprus?</h3>
<p>Cyprus has a favorable tax system, with a flat rate of 12.5% for corporate tax and a maximum of 35% for personal income tax. Retirees can also benefit from certain tax exemptions and reductions. It is important to consult a qualified tax professional for personalized advice.</p>
